Where users stall

Three setup barriers explain most failed activations

31% cannot map their first data source without help. 24% stop at permissions. 19% never invite a teammate. Interview evidence suggests terminology, unclear ownership, delayed data availability, security review, missing examples, and uncertainty about the first useful workflow all contribute. Enterprise administrators experience the longest delays, while small teams progress faster.

Recommendation: create a guided setup path focused on data mapping, permission readiness, and the first shared workflow.
Source: funnel events and 18 onboarding interviews

Decision

Fund a six-week guided-setup pilot

Assign one product designer and two engineers. Measure successful data mapping, time to first shared workflow, and week-4 retention. Keep the existing self-serve path as the control.

Approve the pilot by 31 July 2026; review evidence on 18 September.
Owner: Growth product team

Guardrails

What would change our mind?

Stop if setup time does not improve by at least 20%, support contacts rise by more than 10%, or the pilot causes a measurable permissions-error increase. Do not declare a retention win without the full week-4 window.
